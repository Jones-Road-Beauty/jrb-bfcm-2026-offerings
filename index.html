BFCM 2026 Offer Overview

The complete promotional structure for Black Friday / Cyber Week 2026 — sitewide sale mechanics, the limited-time Minis launch, and the Mini bundle savings that carry through end of year.
Updated Aug 10, 2026
At a Glance

The sitewide sale runs Tue 11/17 through Cyber Monday (Mon 11/30) at 11:59 PM — 15% off everything, upgraded to 20% off on orders of $100+, with Holiday Kits excluded.

On Black Friday (11/27) we introduce limited-time Minis, which count toward the sitewide discounts. The day after Cyber Monday — Tue 12/1 — Minis shift to bundle pricing — 15% off a set of 3, 20% off a set of 5, 25% off a set of 7 — which stays live through 12/31/26.

Notes & Fine Print

  • Holiday Kits are excluded from the sitewide sale for its entire run (11/17–11/30).
  • Minis are limited-time. They are not part of the regular assortment — they launch Black Friday (11/27) and are sold for a limited time.
  • The offers hand off cleanly: sitewide discounts run through 11:59 PM on Cyber Monday (11/30); Mini bundle pricing takes over the next day, Tuesday 12/1. Minis never carry both mechanics at once.
  • Cyber Monday is a sitewide sale day, not a bundle day. The tiered Mini bundle savings (3 / 5 / 7 sets) don't begin until 12/1 — from then on they are the only offer running.
  • Bundle savings outlast Cyber Week — they remain live 12/1 through 12/31/26.
