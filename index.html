BFCM 2026 Offer Overview

The complete promotional structure for Black Friday / Cyber Week 2026 — the sitewide sale, the Mini Bundle Builder and its Travel Bag gift, and the December tiered gift-with-purchase.
Updated Aug 18, 2026
At a Glance

The sitewide sale runs Tue 11/17 through Cyber Monday (Mon 11/30) at 11:59 PM — 15% off everything, upgraded to 20% off on orders of $100+. Holiday Kits and all sets, bundles, and kits are excluded and do not count toward the $100 tier.

On Tue 11/24, limited-time Minis launch with the Bundle Builder discount live from day one — 20% off a set of 3, 25% off a set of 5, 30% off a set of 7 — running through 12/31/26. There is no window where Minis sell at full price. Buy 5+ Minis and get a free Mini Travel Bag (also sold on its own through end of year).

From 12/3 through 12/17, a tiered gift-with-purchase runs: spend $85 for a Reusable Eye Mask, spend $100 for a Refillable Ring — and at $100 the tiers stack, so you get both.

Notes & Fine Print

  • Sets, bundles, and kits are fully outside the sitewide sale — including Holiday Kits and Mini bundles. They don't receive the 15% / 20% discounts and don't count toward reaching the $100 tier.
  • Minis never sell at full price. They launch Tue 11/24 with Bundle Builder pricing live from day one, and the builder runs through 12/31/26.
  • The offers overlap: from 11/24 through 11/30, the sitewide sale and the Bundle Builder run in parallel as separate mechanics — Minis are priced by the builder only.
  • Mini Travel Bag, two ways: free as a GWP on any 5+ Mini purchase, and available as a standalone purchase — both through end of year.
  • Tiered GWP (12/3–12/17) stacks at the top tier: $85 earns the Reusable Eye Mask, $100 earns the Refillable Ring plus the Eye Mask — a $100+ order gets both gifts.
  • Mini bundle spend counts toward the GWP thresholds. Unlike the sitewide sale's $100 tier, the December $85 / $100 gift thresholds do include Mini Bundle Builder purchases.
  • Sitewide sale timing is unchanged: Tue 11/17 through Cyber Monday 11/30 at 11:59 PM.
